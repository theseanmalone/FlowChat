# Sell by Doc — Embedded Video Script
**Sean on camera, direct to lens, no screen recording**
**Target length: 90 seconds – 2 minutes MAX**
**Tone: One human talking to one human. Warm, direct, no pitch energy.**

---

## PRODUCTION NOTES

- Film direct to camera, slightly leaning forward
- Plain background or simple branded backdrop
- No intro music — start mid-sentence if possible
- This video lives INSIDE the sell by doc, above the fold
- The viewer just got sent this doc by Sean or a team member in a DM — treat it that way
- DO NOT say "welcome" or use their name (it's a recorded video, not personalized)
- End with a clear click action pointing DOWN toward the doc content

---

## SCRIPT

---

*[Start mid-lean, relaxed eye contact, slight smile]*

"Hey — if someone sent you this, it's because they thought you'd be a good fit for what we're building inside FlowChat this month.

So let me just tell you exactly what this is in about ninety seconds.

FlowChat is a prospecting platform. It connects to Facebook, LinkedIn, Instagram, and X — and it lets you pull qualified leads from all four of them into one inbox, so you can actually work your pipeline instead of spending all day hunting for people to talk to.

When you join this month, we set the whole thing up FOR you. Four pre-built lead mining pipelines. Four conversation pipelines. Video walkthroughs for every platform. And a private implementation call with our team to make sure it's dialed in for your specific business.

Most people are live and getting leads within 15 minutes of joining.

If you're good at DMs — this system will make you dangerous.

And if you're not sure what to say in the DMs, or how to close once you've got people in your pipeline — that's what the upgrade is for. But we'll talk about that on your call.

Read through what's below. Everything is laid out — what you get, what it costs, and exactly what happens when you join.

I'll see you inside."*

*[Natural end — no "click the link below" — the doc handles the CTA]*

---

## DIRECTOR NOTES

- Keep the energy conversational — not excited, not flat. Like you're telling a friend something useful.
- The line "if you're good at DMs — this system will make you dangerous" should land with a slight smile and pause. That's the money line.
- "I'll see you inside" — look directly at camera, genuine. Not salesy.
- Film 3 takes minimum. Use the one that feels most like a real conversation.

---

## THUMBNAIL / PREVIEW FRAME

The video preview frame (what shows before they press play) should be:
- Sean's face, direct to camera, slight confident smile
- Text overlay: **"Watch this first — 90 sec"**
- This signals: low commitment, worth it
