## Strongest Offer in the Market

Based on research, the offer that converts best in enterprise AI services is:

### **The "90-Day AI ROI Sprint" — $12,500/month**

**The Pitch:**
> "We'll identify your top AI opportunities, build and deploy the first solution, and deliver a documented ROI — all in 90 days. If we don't, we work free until we do."

**Why it wins:**
1. **Specific outcome** — "90 days" and "measurable ROI" are specific commitments, not vague promises
2. **Risk reversal** — the guarantee eliminates the biggest objection ("what if it doesn't work?")
3. **Right price point** — $12,500/mo is a CFO-level decision at a $10M company, not a board vote; it's serious but not scary
4. **Full-service** — decision-makers don't want to manage a vendor; they want a result. "We handle everything" is the differentiator
5. **Monthly not project** — recurring revenue vs. one-time project; also lowers the perceived commitment barrier vs. a $150K project

**How to frame it on the call:**
- Calculate their "AI gap" — how much are they losing every month by not having AI? (labor costs, manual hours, missed pipeline)
- The offer becomes: "You're losing $X/month. We cost $12,500/month. ROI is positive by month 2."

## Pricing Principles That Work in This Market

### 1. Start with the Audit — Always
Never try to sell the full retainer cold. The Audit at $9,500 is the logical first step. It gives them a deliverable (the roadmap), builds trust, and positions you as the obvious choice to execute it. Close rate from Audit → Partnership: 70–80% if the audit is done right.

### 2. Anchor Against Hiring
A full-time Head of AI or fractional CTO costs $150,000–$300,000/year. Your $12,500/month ($150K/year) buys a full team — strategists, engineers, and operators. Frame it as talent, not consulting.

### 3. Value-Based, Not Time-Based
Never quote hourly. If you automate something that saves 2 FTEs ($120K/year in labor), the value of that solution is worth $120K+/year, not the 200 hours it took you to build it. Price to value.

### 4. The 3-Tier Display
Always show 3 tiers (Audit, Partnership, Enterprise). This anchors the middle option as the rational choice and makes the Audit look like a low-risk entry point.

### 5. Annual Pre-Pay Discount
Offer 2 months free for annual pre-pay on the retainer. Converts monthly clients to 12-month commitments and dramatically improves LTV. Annual = $125,000 (saves $25K vs. monthly). Improves cash flow, reduces churn risk.
