## SECTION 1: COMPETITOR RESEARCH
### How the Best Software Demo/Conversion Videos Are Built

**What the top tools do on YouTube:**

| Tool | Video Strategy | What Works |
|---|---|---|
| Dripify | Tutorials + comparison videos | "How I got X leads" framing; viewer is the hero |
| Expandi | Founder-face hooks, screen demos | Emotional opener → fast product walkthrough → urgency CTA |
| MeetAlfred | Feature walkthroughs | Pain-first framing ("tired of manual outreach?") |
| Syndr.ai | Problem-aware hooks | Statistics up front, demo in middle |
| Devi AI | Keyword monitoring demos | Specific use cases shown fast, CTA to free trial |
| PhantomBuster | Step-by-step tutorials | Numbered steps build trust; "by the end of this video" promise |

**What the winners have in common:**
1. **Hook is a pain or a result** — never a feature. "I built 500 leads in 20 minutes" not "FlowChat has an import feature."
2. **Founder/real person on camera** — not screen recordings alone. Face = trust = conversion.
3. **Demo shows the OUTCOME** (leads imported, pipeline built) not just the button clicks.
4. **CTA is specific and low-friction** — "link below, free trial, no card needed."
5. **Thumbnails show a face + a result number** — "500 leads" or "20 min" in big text.
6. **Video length: 4–8 minutes** for YouTube demo/conversion (long enough to sell, short enough to hold attention).

**The gap FlowChat owns:** Every competitor is LinkedIn-focused. Facebook prospecting at scale — especially from groups, comments, polls, events — is largely unclaimed territory on YouTube. This is a blue ocean.
