# DM Sequence — Lead to Sell by Doc
**For all 4 platforms: FB, LinkedIn, IG, X**
**Goal: Get them from first contact → reading the Sell by Doc in 3–5 messages**

---

## THE GOLDEN RULE

Never send the doc cold. Always have at least one real exchange first.
The doc feels like a gift when they're curious. It feels like spam when they're not.

The sequence: **Connect → Curiosity → Conversation → Send the doc**

---

## PLATFORM-SPECIFIC OPENERS

Each platform has a different vibe. Match it.

---

### FACEBOOK

**Best sources:** Group members, post commenters, event attendees

**Opening message (send as a connection/DM, not a pitch):**

> "Hey [Name] — saw you in [Group Name]. What kind of business are you running these days?"

**If they commented on a post:**
> "Hey [Name] — your comment on [post topic] caught my eye. Are you actively building your [coaching/agency/consulting] business right now?"

**If they RSVPd to an event:**
> "Hey [Name] — saw you're going to [Event]. What's your focus right now — more clients, better pipeline, or something else?"

---

### LINKEDIN

**Best sources:** 2nd/3rd connections, post engagers, group members

**Opening message:**

> "Hey [Name] — came across your profile and your work in [space/industry] looks interesting. Quick question — are you doing your own business development right now, or do you have a team on it?"

**If they liked/commented on a post:**
> "Hey [Name] — you engaged with my post about [topic]. Curious — is that something you're actively working on in your business?"

**Note for LinkedIn:** A bit more professional tone. Don't be too casual up front.

---

### INSTAGRAM

**Best sources:** Followers, reel commenters, story viewers

**Opening message:**

> "Hey [Name]! Noticed you follow me / saw your comment on [post]. Are you building something right now or just exploring?"

**If they commented:**
> "[Name] — I saw your comment, love the energy. What are you working on?"

**Instagram tone:** Casual, energetic, brief. Match the platform.

---

### X (TWITTER)

**Best sources:** Followers, reply engagers, list members

**Opening message:**

> "Hey [Name] — noticed you follow me. Quick question — are you actively prospecting for clients or mostly inbound?"

**If they replied to a tweet:**
> "[Name] — loved your reply on [tweet topic]. Are you building a biz around this stuff or more of a side thing?"

**X tone:** Direct and short. No fluff. X users are sharp — they spot BS fast.

## THE CONVERSATION FLOW (after the opener)

This works the same on all 4 platforms once you've got a reply.

---

**Step 1 — They reply (any positive response)**

Your response: Acknowledge + one qualifying question

> "Nice — so you're [doing X thing]. What's your biggest bottleneck right now — getting leads, converting conversations, or something else?"

---

**Step 2 — They share their pain**

Common answers:
- "Getting leads consistently"
- "Finding enough people to talk to"
- "Following up — I lose track"
- "I'm prospecting but nothing's converting"
- "I know I need to be more active on social but I don't have a system"

**Your response (pick the one that fits):**

*If they say leads/prospecting issue:*
> "Yeah, that's the #1 thing I hear. I actually built a system for exactly that — runs on FB, LinkedIn, IG, and X at the same time. Would it be okay if I sent you something I put together? It explains it way better than I can in a DM."

*If they say follow-up/organization issue:*
> "That's a pipeline problem — and it's fixable. I built something that handles exactly that across four platforms. Mind if I send you a quick doc? It lays out the whole thing."

*If they say conversion/DM issue:*
> "Interesting — what does your prospecting flow look like right now? Like, how are you finding people and starting conversations?"

*(Get one more reply, then use the send-doc line above)*

---

**Step 3 — Send the doc (after they say yes or show curiosity)**

> "Perfect — here you go: [Sell by Doc link]
>
> There's a short video at the top — I'd start there. Takes about 90 seconds and sets up everything else in the doc.
>
> Read through it and let me know what questions you have. I check messages here pretty regularly."

---

**Step 4 — Follow-up (if no response in 24–48 hours)**

> "Hey [Name] — did you get a chance to look at that link I sent? Curious what you thought."

*(Send once. If no response after that — move on. Don't double-follow-up.)*

---

**Step 5 — After they read the doc (if they reply with interest)**

> "Glad it made sense. The $147 offer is the lowest I've ever done this — it's specifically for people I'm reaching out to directly. If you grab it, your implementation call is where we dial in the system for your specific business. Any questions before you pull the trigger?"

## OBJECTION HANDLING IN DMs

**"How much is it?"**
> "It's $147/mo for the first two months, then $247/mo — but the doc explains what comes with it way better than I can here. Did you see the link I sent?"

**"I already use [other tool]"**
> "Totally makes sense. This is different in one key way — it runs across Facebook, LinkedIn, Instagram, and X from one place, and it's built for DM conversations specifically, not automated blasting. The doc shows exactly how it works. Worth a look if you're spending any real time on social."

**"I don't have time to set it up"**
> "That's actually the point of the implementation call — we do it with you. Most people are live in under 15 minutes per platform. The call makes sure it's done right."

**"Does it really work?"**
> "Honest answer: it works if you work it. If you're willing to actually have conversations in the DMs, this gives you more qualified people to have them with. The doc has more context — did you get a chance to read it?"

**"I'll think about it"**
> "Of course — take your time. The only thing I'd say is the $147 offer is tied to this specific cohort. Once it fills up, it goes back to $247. No pressure either way — just didn't want you to miss it if you were interested."

## DAILY PROSPECTING RHYTHM

**Per platform, per day:**

| Platform | Action | Volume |
|---|---|---|
| Facebook | Mine 1 group or 1 post's comments | 20–30 leads/day |
| LinkedIn | Mine post engagers or connections | 15–20 leads/day |
| Instagram | Mine reel commenters or followers | 15–20 leads/day |
| X | Mine followers or reply threads | 10–15 leads/day |

**Total new leads/day:** 60–85
**Conversations started/day:** 15–25 (not everyone replies)
**Docs sent/day (realistic):** 3–8
**Close rate on docs (industry avg 5–15%):** 0.15–1.2 sales/day

**At 30 days:** 90–250 docs sent → 4–37 sales (conservative to optimistic)
**30-sale target requires:** ~5–8% close rate on docs sent — very achievable with warm, qualified leads

## NOTES ON TONE

- Never pitch in the first message. Ever.
- The goal of message 1 is to get message 2. That's it.
- The goal of the doc is to pre-sell. Your job in DMs is just to get them curious enough to click.
- Keep messages short — 1–3 sentences max until they're engaged.
- Match the platform energy: LinkedIn = professional, IG = casual, FB = conversational, X = sharp.
- You're a founder talking to another founder. Not a sales rep running a script.
