## Two Videos — Two Jobs — Two Audiences

| | Sell by Doc Video | Sales Page Video |
|---|---|---|
| **Length** | 90 seconds – 2 minutes | Under 7 minutes |
| **Style** | Personal, direct to camera, no screen | Setup demo + offer stack, screen + face |
| **Job** | Build trust, create desire, get the click | Show the system working, close the sale |
| **CTA** | "Click below to see exactly how it works" | "Grab your membership now" |
| **Traffic source** | DMs (warm) | Sales page (warm DM + eventual paid YouTube) |
| **Tone** | Sean talking to ONE person | Sean presenting to a buyer who's ready |
