5
Voice + Writing Rules How every piece of copy sounds — do's and don'ts

Always Do

  • Short sentences. One idea per line.
  • Conversational — sounds like a text, not a brochure
  • Lead with the outcome, not the feature
  • Name the problem before the solution
  • End with one clear next step
  • Use the prospect's first name once per message

Never Do

  • "I hope this email finds you well"
  • Three CTAs in one message
  • Corporate buzzwords (leverage, synergy, scalable)
  • Vague subject lines ("Checking in…")
  • Asking for time before giving value
  • Emojis unless the relationship is warm
## VOICE + WRITING RULES Tone: [e.g. Direct, warm, professional. Sounds like a trusted advisor — not a pitch deck.] Writing style: - Short paragraphs. Max 2–3 sentences. - No fluff openers. Get to the point in line 1. - Active voice. Cut passive constructions. - Contractions are fine: "you're", "we've", "it's" - Use the word "you" often. Focus on their world, not ours. Words we use: [e.g. pipeline, close, revenue, booked, system, flow] Words we avoid: [e.g. blast, crush it, game-changer, holistic, disruptive] Sign-off style: [e.g. "— [First Name]" — no "Best regards," no "Warmly,"] Subject line formula: [e.g. Direct question / Specific outcome / First name included]
