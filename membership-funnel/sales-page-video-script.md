# Sales Page Video Script
**Lives on the checkout page / sales page**
**Target: Under 7 minutes**
**Format: Sean on camera for open/close, screen demo in the middle**
**Audience: Warm lead who read the sell by doc AND cold paid YouTube traffic (eventually)**
**This video must work for BOTH audiences — design for the cold viewer**

## PRODUCTION NOTES

**Screen demo section (sections 3–7):**
- Use a real FlowChat account with actual data — not an empty demo account
- Show leads actually loading in real time — that moment is the "wow"
- Keep the cursor moving slowly — don't rush through the screen sections
- Add light background music under the screen sections only (not under Sean's face sections)

**Sean's on-camera sections:**
- Clean solid shirt, well-lit, direct eye contact
- Hook (section 1) energy: urgent but calm — "I've got something you need to see"
- Offer stack (section 8) energy: confident and generous — not hard-sell
- Close (section 9) energy: warm and direct — you're inviting them in

**Editing:**
- Hard cut between Sean and screen — no fades on transitions
- Add subtle lower-third text during the offer stack: each bullet appears as Sean says it
- DO NOT add intro animation or logo bumper — it kills momentum at the start

**The one rule for this video:**
It must work for someone who has NEVER heard of FlowChat AND for someone who just read the Sell by Doc. Write the hook for the cold viewer. The warm viewer will stay either way.
