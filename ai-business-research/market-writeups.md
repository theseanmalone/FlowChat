# AI Business Market Research — 4 Service Areas
**Target Market: Companies doing $10M+ revenue | Date: May 2026**

---

## 1. AI Agent Development

### What It Is
Custom-built autonomous AI agents that handle multi-step business workflows end-to-end — think AI that can research prospects, write proposals, process orders, manage CRM data, handle customer support, or run internal operations without human intervention. Built specifically to the client's systems (Salesforce, SAP, HubSpot, etc.).

### What Gets Delivered
- Discovery & workflow mapping (2–4 weeks)
- Custom agent architecture + integrations (CRM, ERP, internal tools)
- Testing, QA, and deployment
- Ongoing monitoring, maintenance, and iteration
- Staff training and documentation

**Examples of what agents do:** Autonomous SDR agents, order fulfillment automation, inventory alert agents, AI customer success agents, internal ops agents (HR, finance, reporting)

### Pricing in the Market
| Tier | Price | What's Included |
|---|---|---|
| Simple Agent (1–2 integrations) | $15,000–$50,000 | Single workflow, 1 tool integration |
| Mid-Complexity (3–5 integrations) | $50,000–$150,000 | Multi-step workflow, CRM/ERP connected |
| Enterprise Custom | $150,000–$500,000+ | Multi-agent system, full enterprise stack |
| Monthly Maintenance Retainer | $2,000–$10,000/mo | Monitoring, optimization, updates |

Annual maintenance = 15–25% of initial build cost.

### Top 3 Buyer Types
1. **Operations Leaders at mid-market manufacturers/distributors ($20M–$200M revenue)** — drowning in manual data entry, order processing, and reporting. They want AI that plugs into their ERP and eliminates 10+ hours/week of grunt work per department.
2. **VP of Sales / CRO at B2B SaaS companies** — want AI SDR agents that handle top-of-funnel research, outreach sequencing, and CRM logging so human reps only touch qualified conversations.
3. **E-commerce & retail operators** — need inventory management agents, fulfillment routing agents, and customer service bots that reduce headcount while scaling order volume.

### Competitive Landscape
Key players: Turing, Accenture AI Studios, Thoughtworks, boutique AI dev shops. Boutiques undercut by 40–60% and deliver faster (4–12 weeks vs. 12–24 months for Big 4).

## 2. Managed AI Cybersecurity

### What It Is
Ongoing, outsourced AI-powered security operations — threat detection, response, vulnerability monitoring, and compliance management — delivered as a managed service so the client's team doesn't need to build an in-house SOC. The AI layer processes millions of events in real-time and escalates only what matters.

### What Gets Delivered
- AI-powered Security Operations Center (SOC) as a service
- 24/7 threat monitoring and anomaly detection
- Automated incident response and triage
- Vulnerability scanning and patch prioritization
- Compliance reporting (SOC 2, HIPAA, ISO 27001)
- Monthly executive risk summaries

### Pricing in the Market
| Tier | Price | What's Included |
|---|---|---|
| Starter MDR | $3,000–$8,000/mo | Endpoint monitoring, basic threat detection |
| Full Managed SOC | $8,000–$25,000/mo | 24/7 SOC, incident response, compliance |
| Enterprise MSSP | $25,000–$100,000+/mo | Full stack, dedicated team, custom SLAs |
| Incident Response (one-time) | $15,000–$75,000 | Breach investigation and remediation |

### Top 3 Buyer Types
1. **CFOs/COOs at financial services firms ($10M–$500M)** — regulatory compliance requirements (SOC 2, PCI-DSS) make outsourced monitoring non-negotiable. They can't afford a full in-house team but can't afford a breach either.
2. **CTOs at healthcare/medical companies** — HIPAA liability exposure makes AI-powered monitoring a business necessity, not a luxury. One breach = catastrophic fine + reputational damage.
3. **Private equity-backed companies pre-exit** — need clean security posture for due diligence. PE firms increasingly require SOC 2 compliance as a condition of investment or acquisition.

### Competitive Landscape
Key players: Arctic Wolf, CrowdStrike Falcon Complete, Stellar Cyber, Exaforce, Huntress. Mid-market MSPs are white-labeling these platforms and reselling. High barrier to entry; deep technical expertise required.

## 3. AI Consulting

### What It Is
Strategic advisory and hands-on implementation support helping $10M+ companies identify where AI will drive the most ROI, build the roadmap, select the right tools, and execute. Delivered via retained engagements, workshops, and fractional AI leadership. This is the "front door" service — most companies need this before they can buy anything else.

### What Gets Delivered
- AI readiness audit (systems, data, workflows, team)
- Revenue and cost impact analysis (where's the $?)
- Tool/vendor selection and evaluation
- 90-day AI implementation roadmap
- Change management and staff training
- Ongoing fractional Chief AI Officer (CAIO) support
- KPI dashboards and ROI measurement

### Pricing in the Market
| Tier | Price | What's Included |
|---|---|---|
| AI Audit / Workshop | $5,000–$25,000 (one-time) | 1–3 day deep-dive, deliverable roadmap |
| Essential Retainer | $2,000–$5,000/mo | 5–10 hrs/mo advisory |
| Standard Retainer | $5,000–$15,000/mo | 10–25 hrs/mo, roadmap execution |
| Full Partnership / Fractional CAIO | $15,000–$50,000/mo | 25+ hrs/mo, strategic + operational |

The sweet spot for $10M–$50M companies: $8,000–$20,000/month retainer.

### Top 3 Buyer Types
1. **Founders/CEOs of service businesses ($10M–$50M)** — know AI is important, have no idea where to start, don't want to hire a full-time AI person yet. Want someone to tell them exactly what to do. Best entry point buyer.
2. **COOs at logistics, manufacturing, and distribution companies** — process-heavy operations with clear automation ROI. Want AI to cut labor costs, speed up reporting, and reduce errors. Numbers-driven, easy to build an ROI case for.
3. **Marketing/Revenue leaders at B2B companies** — focused on AI for pipeline generation, content at scale, and sales intelligence. Willing to pay for strategy that connects AI tools to revenue outcomes.

### Competitive Landscape
Boutique firms (Dan Cumberland Labs, Leanware, AgentiveAI) undercutting McKinsey/Accenture by 40–60%. Market is $11B in 2025, projected to reach $90B by 2035. **This is the highest-volume, lowest-barrier entry point in the AI services market.**

## 4. AI Lead Generation

### What It Is
Done-for-you (or done-with-you) AI-powered outbound lead generation — combining intent data, AI prospect research, personalized multi-channel outreach, and lead qualification to fill client pipelines with sales-ready conversations. Replaces or augments the traditional SDR function.

### What Gets Delivered
- ICP definition and lead list building
- AI-personalized email, LinkedIn, and/or ad campaigns
- Lead scoring and qualification
- CRM integration and reporting
- Booked meetings delivered to client's calendar
- Weekly performance reporting and optimization

### Pricing in the Market
| Model | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Pay-Per-Lead (PPL) | $50–$500/lead | Varies by ICP quality and deal size |
| Monthly Retainer | $4,000–$15,000/mo | Full service, ongoing campaigns |
| Performance-Based | $1,000–$5,000/meeting booked | High-ticket B2B only |
| SDR-as-a-Service | $6,000–$20,000/mo | Full SDR replacement with AI |
| Enterprise ABM Programs | $20,000–$100,000/mo | Full account-based marketing stack |

UnboundB2B pricing example: Starter $4K/mo, Growth $6K/mo.

### Top 3 Buyer Types
1. **VP Sales / Head of Revenue at B2B SaaS ($5M–$50M ARR)** — have a quota, need pipeline, can't hire fast enough. AI lead gen is a faster path to booked demos than recruiting 3 SDRs. High urgency, clear ROI.
2. **Founders of professional services firms (consulting, agencies, law, accounting)** — hate cold outreach, have no sales team, need a system that runs without them. Want meetings booked, not a list of names.
3. **Sales directors at mid-market companies launching a new product or expanding to a new vertical** — need to build a new pipeline quickly without rebuilding the sales org. One-time campaign buyers who often become long-term retainer clients.

### Competitive Landscape
Key players: UnboundB2B, Callbox, B2B Rocket, Vector Agents, Intent Amplify, ai-bees. Market growing from $1.7B (2023) to $9.5B by 2030. Competitive but high repeat-purchase rates — clients stay if the meetings convert.
